# gpuicn v0.11.7

Tabs that can carry counts, found in karyalabs/matty's sidebar, where teams
became named tabs with their own mention counts. There are no breaking
changes. The runtime pins are unchanged: GPUI Kit 0.9.1 on `gpui-pre` 0.3.12,
and gpui-icons 0.7.0.

## Added

- **`Tab::trailing`** draws an element after a tab's label, such as a count
  badge or an unread dot, and **`Tab::aria_label`** lets the tab's
  accessible name say what that element shows ("Design, 2 mentions").
- **`Tab::on_secondary_click`** runs a handler on a right click, to open a
  tab's context menu.
- **`Tabs::end`** draws an element after the tabs in the same row, such as a
  "More" menu for tabs that do not fit or an add button.
- **`Tabs::size(TabsSize::Sm)`** draws 13 px labels in a slightly shorter
  row, for sidebars and toolbars.
- **`Tabs::text_colors`** sets the selected and other labels' colors (and
  the line under the selected tab), for tabs on a surface with its own
  colors, such as a sidebar.
