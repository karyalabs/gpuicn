# gpuicn v0.10.1

Adds the pieces a settings window and a team switcher need (karyalabs/matty#731).
No breaking changes; the runtime pins are unchanged: GPUI Kit 0.9.0 on
`gpui-pre` 0.3.11 and gpui-icons 0.6.0.

## Added

- **Rich menu items** (#174). `MenuItem` takes `.icon(LucideIcon)` or a
  caller-built `.leading(..)` element, a muted `.description(..)` line, a
  `.trailing(..)` slot for counts and dots, and a `.shortcut(..)` written like
  a key binding (`"secondary-alt-1"` draws `⌘⌥1` on macOS, `Ctrl+Alt+1`
  elsewhere). Rows with an icon or a description are 36px tall; a checked
  item's check sits after the trailing slot. `MenuState::width` fixes the
  root panel's width.

  ```rust
  MenuItem::new("tech", "Tech")
      .leading(|_, cx| team_tile("T", cx))
      .trailing(|_, cx| mention_pill(3, cx))
      .shortcut("secondary-alt-1")
      .radio(true)
  ```

- **`Item` and `ItemGroup`** (#175), a port of shadcn/ui's `item`: a row with
  media, a title, a description clamped to two lines and actions at the
  trailing edge, or under the copy with `.stacked(true)`. Variants `Default`,
  `Outline` and `Muted`, sizes `Default` (48px) and `Sm`, and `.on_click` for
  rows that open something. `ItemGroup` is one bordered card with hairline
  `ItemSeparator`s between its items.

  ```rust
  ItemGroup::new("startup").child(
      Item::new("open-at-login")
          .title("Open at login")
          .description("Start Matty when you sign in")
          .actions(Switch::new("open-at-login.switch").checked(on)),
  )
  ```
