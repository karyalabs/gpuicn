# gpuicn v0.11.1

Pieces for karyalabs/matty's full-height window and its redesigned sidebar
(no title bar; the sidebar and the pane headers are the window's top edge).
No breaking changes; the runtime pins are unchanged: GPUI Kit 0.9.1 on
`gpui-pre` 0.3.12 and gpui-icons 0.7.0.

## Added

- **`title_bar::window_drag_area(id, element, window, cx)`** makes any
  stateful element drag the window as `TitleBar` does: dragging empty space
  moves the window, a double-click zooms it (macOS follows the user's
  titlebar double-click setting), and a right-click on client-decorated
  Linux opens the window menu. It does nothing on the web.
- **`title_bar::WindowControls`**: the minimize, maximize or restore, and
  close controls on their own, for the end of a pane header. It renders
  nothing where the platform draws its own (macOS, server-decorated Linux,
  the web). `on_close` works as on `TitleBar`.
- **`title_bar::draws_window_controls(window)`** and
  **`traffic_light_inset(window)`** (80px on macOS outside full screen).
- **`Sidebar::track_scroll`** shares the navigation's scroll position with
  the caller, also under `roving_focus`.
- **`Sidebar::overlay`** floats a child over the scrolling navigation, such
  as an "N unread below" pill.
- **`Sidebar::header_padding`** and **`Sidebar::footer_padding`** override
  the 8px inset of the header and footer.

## Changed

- `TitleBar` is built from `window_drag_area` and `WindowControls`; it looks
  and behaves as before.
